### Bibliographic identifiers

ISBN, UDC (УДК), BBK (ББК), and the Russian author mark have **not** yet been assigned. The placeholders in the layout are intentional. For a U.S. publication, ISBN is obtained from Bowker; UDC/BBK/author mark are Russian-library publishing metadata and should be assigned through an authoritative bibliographic workflow rather than guessed.

This is a release candidate, not yet the printer-specific final production package.
